(draft PR #24, do not merge), `main` plus PRs #8–#28; see #24 for the exact head and its CI. Rubric:
codereview1 rev 2. Each check scores 0 (absent, failing or unverified), 1 (implemented
with partial evidence) or 2 (demonstrated on the candidate). Checks marked `*` are
