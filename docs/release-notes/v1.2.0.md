# TrueNorth Range v1.2.0: known limitations, simulator-only evidence and staging record

What changed is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md) (v1.2.0). How a release is built,
signed and verified is in [`docs/release.md`](../release.md). This page covers what does not
work yet, what is proven only against simulators, and what was verified on staging.

Written 2026-10-11 against `v1.2.0-rc3` (on staging) and the release-readiness branch
`claude/release-ready-gaps`.

## Known limitations in v1.2.0

### At a glance

Checked against the code on 2026-10-12. Details follow below or in the linked documents.

- **Injects are simulated.** Every scenario injector writes synthetic telemetry (no network,
  process or file activity on range hosts); the one exception is the Greyspace breadcrumb,
  which is simulator-proven only (`docs/scenario-inject-execution.md`).
- **No host sensors.** Provisioning installs no telemetry sensor (Beats or similar) on range
  VMs; range telemetry comes from the injectors, the noise engine and the ingest API.
- **No team exercises or leaderboards** (planned, R2). Teams can be created and given
  members (API), but exercises take no teams, there is one score per exercise, and there
  are no leaderboards or team standings (`docs/user-guide.md`, "Not in v1.2.0").
- **No global keyboard shortcuts** or command palette (Range Designer shortcuts only).
- **English only.** No i18n and no French interface; French is a planned milestone after R1.
- **Encryption is partial** (`docs/security.md`, "Encryption" and "Compliance Mapping"): no
  KMS or Vault, no database or disk encryption (only sealed credential columns), and
  internal service traffic is not encrypted.
- **Alertmanager delivery is unproven**, the **nightly backup does not stop the ARC² runner**
  (see [Operations](#operations)), **abandoning a range leaves its VMs and port groups** on
  the vCenter (see below), and **vSphere is simulator-proven only**.

### vSphere work is proven on simulators only

There is still no run against a real vCenter. The live lab workflow (`lab.yml`) is manual
only until its self-hosted runner is registered (`docs/runbooks/lab-runner.md`), and
staging has no vCenter (a simulated, read-only one for the dashboards). See
[Simulator-proven only](#simulator-proven-only) for the list.

### Directory sign-in: Samba-tested, not yet Windows AD / AD CS qualified

Two separate claims, recorded separately:

| Claim | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Samba-tested** | Yes (CI job `ad`) | A Samba 4.17 AD DC with its own test CA (`tools/ad-test-dc`), federated by the installer's own Keycloak tasks; `tests/integration/test_ad_directory.py` in fixture mode |
| **Windows AD DS / AD CS qualified** | **No** | Pending the same tests in external mode against the owner's Windows DC on staging (M2 run), then the work domain at cut-over |

What the Samba run established, and which holds until Windows says otherwise:

- **Nested groups are not expanded**: only direct membership of a TN-* group counts.
- **Removing someone from a group does not remove their TrueNorth role.** AD groups suggest
  a role at registration; the role is granted by approval and stays until changed in
  TrueNorth. The `groups` claim itself drops within the changed-users sync interval
  (300 s; measured 93 s and 287 s on two runs), plus up to 5 min for tokens already issued.
- **Disabling an AD account** stops new sign-ins at once and refreshes within 300 s.
- **An AD account sharing a local account's email cannot sign in** (Keycloak refuses the
  import and reports it as a failed sync); while one exists, the Keycloak admin console's
  user list fails. Fix the address on one side.
- **MFA for AD staff is not tested**: the MFA policy (S-track) is not in this release; the
  lane carries a skipped TODO for it.
- Samba is not Windows: AD CS certificate templates, Kerberos/LDAP signing policy,
  `userAccountControl` variants (expired, locked out, password-must-change) and
  multi-DC replication delay are not exercised.

### vSphere connections and ranges built before connection records

Since the hardening of #131 every range records the vCenter connection it was built on
(`provisioner_output.connection`) and later operations reach only that one. Ranges built
before then record theirs the first time an operation (destroy, power, a snapshot, the
scheduled health check) finds one of their VMs. Limits:

- **Port groups only:** a pre-record range with port groups but no VM on record (a build
  that died before its first VM) has nothing to confirm by. It keeps following the tenant's
  preferred connection until it is destroyed, and the API refuses connection changes that
  would move it (409) until then.
- **VMs gone:** a pre-record range none of whose VMs exist where it resolves fails its
  destroy rather than being recorded destroyed. The tenant may then repoint its connection
  at the vCenter that built it. If the VMs are really gone, a platform administrator can
  `POST /ranges/{id}/abandon` (reason required, audit-logged).
- **Abandon touches nothing:** the range is recorded destroyed, but any VMs and port groups
  still on a vCenter stay there. `provisioner_output.abandoned` and the audit entry list
  them; remove them by hand. Its VLAN reservations become reusable as a destroyed range's do.
- **Shared connections:** a connection with no tenant is shared (used for tenants without
  their own). The tenant API lists and edits only a tenant's own connections, so shared ones
  are managed outside it (database or installer).
- A failed vSphere connection test reports the failure and leaves the connection active.

### ARC² test-host content is unreviewed

On staging the ARC² runner accepts the outline and preview gates by itself
(`ARC2_AUTO_ACCEPT_GATES`, `accepted_by: auto (test host)`, badged "TEST CONTENT"), and the
batch driver accepts each release's open actions. Staging's 45 courses are therefore test
content: never release them to Students or copy them to another host
(`docs/content-library.md`, `docs/arc2-course-studio.md` §13–15).

### C105's release is a fixture, and C105 stays a draft

`content/releases/c105-foundations-d0e83b9f6c1f.tar.gz` is the fixture for the cmi5 API
tests and the CATAPULT lane. The shipped-content load and the ARC² batch no longer publish
C105 (`KEEP_DRAFT`), but a host that published it before keeps it published: unpublish it
by hand.

### cmi5 and LTI

- A cmi5 Moodle activity is tied to one course release.
- An LTI cmi5 launch does not enrol the Student in the TrueNorth course.
- WebSockets do not accept the LTI hand-off session (live updates need a direct
  TrueNorth sign-in).

### Carried over from v1.0.0

- Student telemetry search on range indices created before the v1.0.0 upgrade (workaround
  in `docs/release-notes/v1.0.0.md`).
- Multi-tenant installs must set `PLATFORM_TENANT_ID`.
- LTI needs HTTPS.

Resolved since v1.0.0: staff deep linking (#132) and H6, per-tenant vCenter credentials
(#131, simulator-proven).

### Operations

- The Prometheus alerts for OpenSearch health, Moodle, the ARC² runner and backups (new in
  this branch) are unit-tested with promtool. Alerts can now be delivered by email
  (`tn_alert_email_enabled`; install/README.md "Alerting"). It is off on staging until the SMTP
  details are known, so alerts there still go nowhere, and delivery is not proven until
  `playbooks/alert-test.yml` has run on staging and its message arrived.
- The nightly backup does not stop the ARC² runner: a job running at that moment may be
  captured half-written (recorded in the manifest). Restore needs the runner stopped
  (`docs/runbooks/backup-restore.md`, "ARC² runs").

## Simulator-proven only

Each of these passes in CI against a simulator or fake, and has **not** run against real
infrastructure:

| Feature | Proven against | Lane / tests |
|---|---|---|
| Range provisioning through `vsphere_api` | govmomi vcsim | CI `vsphere-sim` |
| Per-tenant vCenter credentials for every vSphere operation (H6, #131) | vcsim and fakes | CI `vsphere-sim`, worker unit tests |
| VyOS and OPNsense receive the range's rendered config (#131) | vcsim and fakes | worker unit tests |
| Greyspace on vSphere: gs-core VM, `configure_range`, mail/NTP/HTTPS, NPC traffic, breadcrumb inject (#133) | vcsim and the T0 Docker stack | CI `greyspace`, `docs/release-notes/greyspace-vsphere.md` |

Proven against real third-party implementations in CI, not on staging: the cmi5 runtime
(ADL CATAPULT, CI `cmi5`) and Moodle publication (a disposable Moodle, CI `moodle`).
