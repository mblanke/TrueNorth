# Foundation F7 — content and operational exercise validation

Status: blocked until F0/F1/F6 and a named training authority/SMEs are available. Suggested
evidence branch: `claude/foundation-f7-content-validation`.

## Closure outcome

Representative curricula and exercises are instructionally approved, operationally
rehearsed, safely delivered and evidence/scoring calibrated. Test-host ARC² output and course
fixtures never become accredited content merely because software tests pass.

## Governance slice

1. Define authorities and rubrics for QSP/PO/EO traceability, technical accuracy,
   instructional design, bilingual review, accessibility, safety, scoring and annual expiry.
2. Separate statuses: generated/draft, technical review, instructional review, rehearsal,
   approved, published, superseded and withdrawn. Record reviewer/version/provenance.
3. Select three flagship exercises: enterprise defence, hybrid/cloud incident and
   cyber-physical/vehicle/OT event. Declare exact objectives, prerequisites, environment,
   injects, evidence, scoring, safety and reset.
4. Define calibration method for deterministic scoring, instructor judgment, inter-rater
   agreement, appeals/corrections and remediation/retest.

## Rehearsal slices

- Run instructor/operator tabletop, build/preflight, learner pilot, ENDEX/AAR and teardown for
  each flagship on supported infrastructure.
- Validate telemetry/sensors, benign noise, scenario actions, detection evidence, team/process
  observations and missing-evidence behavior.
- Where an approved objective requires real host effects or sensors, route the missing provider
  through bounded scenario/timeline tickets; synthetic inject output remains labelled and does
  not become physical proof merely because the rehearsal otherwise passes.
- Measure preparation time, learner completion, AAR latency, reset/cleanup and capacity.
- Feed defects into bounded code/content tickets; repeat until the approval rubric passes.
- Withdraw fixture/test content from learner visibility and prove published revisions preserve
  historical attempts/results.

## Acceptance

- Every approved objective maps to instruction, practice, observable evidence and assessment.
- SMEs and training authority sign exact versions; model output is never its own approval.
- Scores/outcomes are reproducible within declared policy; disagreements and missing evidence
  remain visible.
- Safety, custody, reset and kill procedures pass rehearsal for physical/adversary effects.
- Student/instructor materials pass the applicable F6 language/accessibility gate.
- AAR/remediation/retest close the loop and historical records remain traceable.

## Recovery

Pilot only with synthetic/approved participants and isolated environments. Stop unsafe or
invalid events, quarantine affected assets/content and preserve evidence. Withdraw rather than
delete content with learner history.

## Skills

Primary: `tn-curriculum-traceability`; add `tn-scenario-evidence`,
`tn-release-verification`, `tn-telemetry-lms` and `tn-docs-handoff`. Humans own accreditation,
safety and instructional acceptance.
